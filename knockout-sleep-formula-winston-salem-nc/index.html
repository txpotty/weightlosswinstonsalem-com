    How the Knockout Sleep Formula Works in Winston-Salem

    Who the Knockout Sleep Formula Is For

    This formula is meant for adults with chronic insomnia who tried good sleep habits and at least one simpler option, like ramelteon or low-dose doxepin, without enough improvement. People with trouble at both ends of the night are the usual fit. It is not a first step. It is best for those who can reliably spend seven to eight hours in bed. Untreated sleep apnea rules it out.
