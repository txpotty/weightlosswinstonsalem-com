    How Tesamorelin Treatment Works in Winston-Salem, NC

    How Tesamorelin Works in Winston-Salem

    Tesamorelin is a stabilized version of growth hormone releasing hormone. After injection it prompts pulsed growth hormone release, IGF-1 rises, and fat breakdown increases, with the biggest effect on visceral fat deep inside the abdomen. Feedback controls stay active. The brand is Egrifta SV, and compounded versions also exist.

    Who Tesamorelin Is Usually For

    The approved use is narrow, excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV who have lipodystrophy. Providers sometimes prescribe it off-label for adults with a large waist, fatty liver or metabolic risk factors when visceral fat is the main problem. Do not expect big scale changes. Pregnancy, active cancer and prior pituitary surgery or head radiation rule it out.

    Tesamorelin Dosing and Schedule in Winston-Salem

    Once a day, into the belly. Egrifta SV uses a fixed 1.4 milligram daily dose given under the skin of the abdomen, while compounded protocols can differ and your provider explains the exact amount. Rotate injection spots around the belly to cut down on redness. Pick the same time daily so it becomes routine.

    What to Expect Over Six Months

    The first month is mostly habit building. Clinical studies judged results at 26 weeks, when visceral fat had fallen by roughly 15 to 18 percent on average. Some people see the waistband loosen around months two and three. With no meaningful change by six months, providers usually recommend stopping.

    Tesamorelin Side Effects in Winston-Salem

    Joint pain is common. Other common complaints are redness or itching at the site, sore muscles and aching arms or legs. Swollen hands or ankles and tingling fingers also happen, and because the drug can push glucose up, people with prediabetes or diabetes need closer monitoring. Rash or hives need prompt attention.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Before prescribing, a provider reviews your history, medications and labs. Baseline testing usually includes A1C or fasting glucose, IGF-1 and a metabolic panel, and people with diabetes may be asked about recent eye exams because of retinopathy risk. Cancer history, pituitary problems and pregnancy plans are covered too. Lab reports can be uploaded as photos.

    Tesamorelin Compared to Related Options in Winston-Salem

    Sermorelin is the closest relative. Both are growth hormone releasing hormone analogs, but tesamorelin is more stable in the body and has trial data for visceral fat while sermorelin does not. CJC-1295 with ipamorelin also stimulates growth hormone but has no FDA-approved use. GLP-1 drugs like semaglutide and tirzepatide lower total body weight through appetite, which is a different job.
