    How Sildenafil Works in Thomasville

    Sildenafil blocks PDE5. PDE5 breaks down cGMP, and cGMP is what relaxes the blood vessels in the penis, so with the enzyme blocked, blood flow during arousal improves. Arousal is still required. The pill does not create desire or an erection out of nowhere. What it does is make an erection easier to reach and to hold.

    Who Sildenafil Is For

    The usual candidate is an adult man with erectile dysfunction who has sex now and then and would rather take a pill only when needed. Causes can be physical, psychological or both. Diabetes, high cholesterol and treated high blood pressure are common in men who respond, and each deserves its own follow-up. Some men end up better suited to tadalafil. Mostly the ones who want a window longer than a few hours.

    Sildenafil Dosing and Timing in Thomasville

    Take it by mouth 30 to 60 minutes before sex. Common strengths are 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg, and most men start at 50 mg. No more than one dose in any 24 hour period. A greasy dinner can push the onset back by an hour, so a lighter meal beforehand helps. The effect usually lasts four to five hours.

    What the First Few Weeks Look Like

    Plenty of men notice a difference with the first dose. Others need several attempts before the timing clicks. A fair trial is four to eight separate attempts at a given dose, because first-time nerves and a couple of drinks can blunt the effect early on. Mild headache and flushing are common early. Keep a quick note of dose, timing and results. That record is what your provider uses to adjust.

    Sildenafil Side Effects in Thomasville

    Headache, facial flushing, a stuffy nose and indigestion are the most common side effects. Some men notice a blue tint to their vision or extra light sensitivity for a few hours. Dizziness is more likely with alcohol, with blood pressure medication and in men who stand up quickly after taking a 100 mg dose. An erection longer than four hours, or any sudden loss of vision or hearing, is an emergency room situation, not a message to your provider. Rare, but worth knowing.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule sildenafil out. Nitroglycerin, isosorbide and poppers are all nitrates, and pairing any of them with sildenafil can crash blood pressure. The pulmonary hypertension drug riociguat is excluded too. Your provider asks about heart attack or stroke in the past six months, unstable angina and severe liver or kidney disease. One more question matters: can you climb two flights of stairs without chest pain?

    Sildenafil Compared to Other ED Options in Thomasville

    Sildenafil kicks in within an hour and fades in about four to five hours. Tadalafil lasts up to 36 hours and also comes in a daily dose. Chewables, troches and rapid dissolve tablets are compounded formats, and compounded products are not FDA-approved. Generic sildenafil is FDA-approved and is chemically the same as Viagra, so it is often where providers start for men who want a short, predictable window. Short window, long track record.
