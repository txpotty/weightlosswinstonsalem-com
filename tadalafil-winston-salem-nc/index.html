    How Tadalafil Works in Winston-Salem

    Tadalafil is a PDE5 inhibitor, the same drug class as sildenafil. With PDE5 blocked, cGMP builds up and relaxes the blood vessel walls in the penis, which lets blood flow in during arousal. It clears slowly, with a half-life near 17.5 hours. That is why one dose can last a day and a half. Arousal is still required.

    Who Tadalafil Is For

    Men with ED who want flexibility tend to like tadalafil. As-needed tadalafil works well for men who want more than the four to five hours sildenafil offers. Daily dosing suits men who have sex twice a week or more. A daily 5 mg tablet can handle ED and the urinary symptoms of BPH at the same time, because tadalafil carries FDA approval for both. Your provider makes the final call.

    Tadalafil Dosing Options in Winston-Salem

    As-needed dosing usually starts at 10 mg, taken 30 minutes to two hours before sex. The range runs from 5 mg to 20 mg. Daily dosing is 2.5 mg or 5 mg, taken at the same time every day with or without food, and it takes about five days to reach a steady level. One dose per day, either way. Unlike sildenafil, a heavy meal barely changes it.

    What to Expect in the First Weeks

    With as-needed tadalafil, most men notice a difference on the first or second try. Daily dosing takes about a week to feel steady. The first week or two can bring headaches, heartburn or a dull lower back ache, and those often fade as you keep taking it. Track what you notice. After about a month, your provider reviews how it is going.

    Tadalafil Side Effects in Winston-Salem

    Headache, indigestion, back pain and muscle aches are the most common side effects. Back aches typically start 12 to 24 hours after taking it and clear within a couple of days. Flushing and a stuffy nose are also common. Get emergency care for any erection over four hours or a sudden drop in vision or hearing, since those are rare but serious. Rare, but real.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates in any form rule out tadalafil, including nitroglycerin and poppers, and so does riociguat. Men with a heart attack in the past 90 days, unstable angina, uncontrolled blood pressure or a stroke in the past six months need careful review. Severe liver or kidney disease changes the dose. With ketoconazole, ritonavir or similar drugs, as-needed tadalafil is capped at 10 mg every 72 hours. Your provider also asks whether you can handle moderate exertion without chest pain.

    Sildenafil works for four to five hours and is slowed by a heavy meal. Tadalafil lasts up to 36 hours and food barely affects it.
