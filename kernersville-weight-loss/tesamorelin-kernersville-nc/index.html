    How Tesamorelin Treatment Works in Kernersville, NC

    How Tesamorelin Works in Kernersville

    Tesamorelin is a stabilized version of growth hormone releasing hormone. It signals the pituitary to release growth hormone in pulses, which raises IGF-1 and pushes the body to break down fat, especially the visceral fat stored around the liver and intestines. Feedback controls stay active. Brand-name Egrifta SV is the approved product, and some pharmacies compound it.

    Who Tesamorelin Is Usually For

    The approved use is narrow, excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV who have lipodystrophy. Off-label, it may be considered for adults whose waist circumference and metabolic labs point to visceral fat as the main issue, sometimes with fatty liver. Do not expect big scale changes. Anyone pregnant, with active cancer, or with a disrupted pituitary axis is not a candidate.

    Tesamorelin Dosing and Schedule in Kernersville

    Once a day, into the belly. Egrifta SV uses a fixed 1.4 milligram daily dose given under the skin of the abdomen, while compounded protocols can differ and your provider explains the exact amount. Moving the injection site each day reduces irritation. Pick the same time daily so it becomes routine.

    What to Expect Over Six Months

    The first month is mostly habit building. Clinical studies judged results at 26 weeks, when visceral fat had fallen by roughly 15 to 18 percent on average. Some people see the waistband loosen around months two and three. With no meaningful change by six months, providers usually recommend stopping.

    Tesamorelin Side Effects in Kernersville

    Joint pain is common. Other common complaints are redness or itching at the site, sore muscles and aching arms or legs. Some people get swelling in the hands or feet or numbness and tingling like carpal tunnel syndrome, and tesamorelin can raise blood sugar, which matters for anyone with prediabetes or diabetes. Report any rash or hives right away.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history, medications and labs. Baseline testing usually includes A1C or fasting glucose, IGF-1 and a metabolic panel, and people with diabetes may be asked about recent eye exams because of retinopathy risk. Cancer history, pituitary problems and pregnancy plans are covered too. Lab reports can be uploaded as photos.

    Tesamorelin Compared to Related Options in Kernersville

    Sermorelin is the closest relative. Both are growth hormone releasing hormone analogs, but tesamorelin is more stable in the body and has trial data for visceral fat while sermorelin does not. CJC-1295 with ipamorelin also stimulates growth hormone but has no FDA-approved use. GLP-1 drugs like semaglutide and tirzepatide lower total body weight through appetite, which is a different job.
