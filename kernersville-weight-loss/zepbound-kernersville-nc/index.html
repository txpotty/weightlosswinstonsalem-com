    How Zepbound Treatment Works in Kernersville, NC

    How Zepbound Works in Kernersville

    Tirzepatide acts on two gut hormone receptors. GIP and GLP-1 together help regulate appetite, slow how fast the stomach empties and improve the way the body handles blood sugar after a meal. Most people feel full on smaller portions. You inject it once weekly into the belly, thigh or upper arm.

    Who Zepbound Is For

    Zepbound is approved for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. Adults with a BMI of 27 or more also qualify if they have a condition tied to weight, such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol or sleep apnea. A separate approval covers moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity. It is not approved for children.

    Zepbound Dosing Schedule in Kernersville

    Everyone starts at 2.5 mg. That first month is about letting your stomach adjust, not about weight loss, so modest early changes are normal. From there, doses climb in 2.5 mg steps every four weeks or more, through 5, 7.5, 10 and 12.5 mg to a top of 15 mg. Most people settle at 5 mg, 10 mg or 15 mg.

    What to Expect Week by Week in Kernersville

    The first month is mostly quiet. Appetite often drops within days, while nausea or an upset stomach shows up most in the day or two after each injection and after every dose increase. The SURMOUNT-1 clinical study reported average weight loss of roughly 20 percent at the 15 mg dose over 72 weeks. Individual results vary.

    Zepbound Side Effects

    Nausea and diarrhea come up most. Vomiting, constipation, indigestion, stomach pain and redness at the injection site are also common, especially during the first days after each dose increase. Serious but less common risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, kidney injury from dehydration and severe allergic reactions. Zepbound carries a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.

    Eligibility and Screening in Kernersville

    Your provider starts with height, weight and BMI. Next comes your medical history and every medication you take, with special attention to insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills. Anyone with medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 in their family is not a candidate, and Zepbound is not used while pregnant. Labs may be requested.

    Zepbound Compared With Related Options in Kernersville

    Zepbound and Mounjaro are the same medicine. Zepbound carries the weight management label and Mounjaro carries the type 2 diabetes label, which matters for insurance and for how your provider writes the prescription. In the SURMOUNT-5 head-to-head trial, tirzepatide produced more average weight loss than Wegovy. Compounded tirzepatide is not FDA-approved and is a different product from Zepbound.
