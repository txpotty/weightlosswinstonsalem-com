    How Flibanserin Treatment Works for Women in Greensboro, NC

    How Flibanserin Works in Greensboro

    Flibanserin acts on serotonin receptors in the brain, not on blood flow and not on hormones. By turning down some serotonin activity, it is thought to give dopamine and norepinephrine, the chemicals linked with interest and motivation, more room to work. It is not a hormone. Viagra works on blood flow, so the two are not comparable. Any change comes gradually, after several weeks of taking it every night.

    Who Flibanserin Is Meant For

    The FDA approval covers premenopausal women with acquired, generalized HSDD. In plain terms, desire used to be normal, it has dropped in most situations and with most partners, and that drop bothers you. Not every dip qualifies. Low desire driven mainly by depression, a medication such as an SSRI, a medical illness or a relationship problem is a different situation. Postmenopausal women are not part of the labeled population.

    Dosing and the Nightly Schedule in Greensboro

    The dose is one 100 mg tablet, once a day, at bedtime. Always at bedtime. Dizziness and sleepiness peak in the hours right after a dose, so taking it before sleep means most of that happens while you are already asleep. This is a daily medication, not something taken before sex. Missed a dose? Skip it, and do not double up the next night.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly about side effects, usually mild sleepiness or dizziness that eases. That part usually passes. Some women notice more interest by week four, and others need the full eight weeks before they can tell. In the clinical trials, the average gain over placebo was modest, about one additional satisfying sexual event a month, along with less distress. Your provider checks in near week eight, and if nothing meaningful has changed, flibanserin is stopped.

    Side Effects of Flibanserin in Greensboro

    The most common side effects are dizziness, sleepiness, nausea and fatigue. Dry mouth and trouble sleeping show up too. The serious risk is a sharp drop in blood pressure that can cause fainting, and it is more likely after alcohol or with drugs that raise flibanserin levels. Do not drive or run machinery for at least six hours after a dose. If you ever feel faint, lie down and contact your provider.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with your full medication list. Flibanserin cannot be combined with moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitors, which include fluconazole, ketoconazole, clarithromycin, several HIV medicines and large amounts of grapefruit juice. Women with any liver impairment cannot take it. Expect questions about past fainting, blood pressure, depression, pregnancy plans and weekly drinks.

    Flibanserin Compared With PT-141 and Other Options in Greensboro

    Flibanserin and bremelanotide, sold as Vyleesi and often called PT-141, are the two FDA-approved medicines for HSDD before menopause. One is a pill every night and the other is a shot only when you want it. PT-141 has no daily alcohol timing, which matters to some women. Compounded options such as oxytocin are not FDA-approved for this use, and your provider will say so. Your provider explains which fits.
