    What Causes Low Libido in Greensboro

    Low libido in women rarely has a single cause. Common contributors include SSRIs and SNRIs, hormonal birth control, perimenopause, thyroid problems, chronic stress, poor sleep, pain during sex and relationship strain. So does how you feel in your body. Persistent, distressing low desire without another explanation is called hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. That label opens up FDA-approved options.

    Who Low Libido Treatment Is For

    This is for adult women bothered by a drop in desire or arousal. Women whose desire faded after a new medication, a baby, perimenopause or a hard year often benefit. No diagnosis is needed first. Women who are simply not bothered by lower desire do not need treatment, since HSDD is defined partly by distress. Your provider helps sort it out.

    Treatment Options and Schedules in Greensboro

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is a 100 mg tablet taken every night at bedtime. PT-141, brand name Vyleesi, is a 1.75 mg self-injection taken at least 45 minutes before sex, with limits of once a day and eight times a month. Oxytocin is a compounded option. It is used off-label as a spray or troche before intimacy and is not FDA-approved. Some women need no medication at all.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly review. Early steps often include adjusting a medication, treating dryness or improving sleep, sometimes alongside a new prescription. PT-141 can work with the first dose. Flibanserin takes four to eight weeks, and it is typically stopped at the eight week mark if there is no benefit. Check-ins track the change.

    Side Effects in Greensboro

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, fatigue and low blood pressure. Those effects are worse with alcohol or CYP3A4-blocking drugs, which is why the dose goes in at bedtime and the label limits drinking near that dose. Nausea is common with PT-141. Flushing, headache, injection site redness, a short blood pressure bump and skin darkening with frequent use are also possible. Oxytocin can cause headache.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Liver disease rules out flibanserin. Drugs like fluconazole, clarithromycin and certain HIV medications rule it out, and drinking has to be kept away from the bedtime dose. PT-141 has different limits. Women with high blood pressure that is not controlled, or with heart disease, should not use PT-141. Neither is used during pregnancy.

    Comparing Low Libido Options in Greensboro

    Flibanserin is daily and steady. It fits women who drink little and like a routine, with clinical trials showing a modest average gain of about one more satisfying encounter a month versus placebo. PT-141 is taken only when wanted. That suits some women, but nausea puts others off. Oxytocin is gentler, with limited research.
