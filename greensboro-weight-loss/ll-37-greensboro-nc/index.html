    How LL-37 Peptide Therapy Works in Greensboro, NC

    How LL-37 Works in Greensboro

    LL-37 is cut from a larger protein called hCAP18, and it is the only cathelicidin the human body makes, named for the 2 leucines at its start and its 37 amino acids. In lab studies it punches holes in bacterial and fungal membranes. It also signals other immune cells and has been studied for wound healing. Mostly preclinical. Early trials looked at topical LL-37 on chronic leg ulcers, but no LL-37 product is FDA-approved.

    Who Usually Asks About LL-37

    Typical candidates are adults with no inflammatory skin disease who have read about innate immune peptides and want a clinician involved before trying one. A few are already seeing someone for gut or skin concerns. Others use BPC-157 or Thymosin Alpha-1 and want the whole plan reviewed. It is not an antibiotic. An active infection still needs standard treatment.

    Dosing and Schedule in Greensboro

    When prescribed, LL-37 is a small injection under the skin, usually in the belly, using an insulin-style needle and a low starting dose measured in micrograms. Many plans start with several doses a week rather than daily. Cycles are short. Most cycles last 4 to 6 weeks before a break, and your label lists the exact dose and days.

    The First Weeks on LL-37

    Week one is about technique and watching your skin, since redness at the site is common and usually fades within a day or two. Nothing dramatic tends to happen early. Check your face. Flushing or new redness on the cheeks is worth a message, and at the end-of-cycle check-in your provider asks what changed and what did not.

    Side Effects and Safety in Greensboro

    Research ties high LL-37 levels to psoriasis and rosacea, and fragments of it can act as a trigger for the immune response in lupus, which is why providers screen so carefully. Injection-site redness and itching are the usual complaints. Some people report mild fatigue. New joint pain or a spreading rash means stopping and sending a message.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The questionnaire asks about psoriasis, rosacea, eczema, lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, pregnancy and every medication you take, including biologics and steroids. A psoriasis or lupus diagnosis usually rules LL-37 out. Pregnancy does too. Some providers request a 25-hydroxy vitamin D level, because low vitamin D lowers natural LL-37 production.

    LL-37 Compared With Other Peptides in Greensboro

    LL-37 works on innate immunity, the fast, nonspecific side, while Thymosin Alpha-1 is discussed for T cells and adaptive immunity, which is the slower, targeted side of the system. For recovery goals, BPC-157 and TB-500 are the usual topics. GHK-Cu is chosen for skin quality. One at a time is easier to judge. Sometimes vitamin D repletion is the simpler first move.
