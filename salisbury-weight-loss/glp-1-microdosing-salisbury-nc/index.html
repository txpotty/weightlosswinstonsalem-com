    How GLP-1 Microdosing Works in Salisbury

    Microdosing is a dosing strategy, not a separate drug. It means taking semaglutide or tirzepatide at doses lower than the standard labeled schedule, or increasing the dose more slowly, while the medicine still works on the same gut hormone receptors. Others notice little change. Low doses usually require compounded medicine, which lacks FDA approval.

    Who GLP-1 Microdosing Is For

    Three groups ask about it most. People who could not tolerate standard GLP-1 doses because of nausea, people who want a slower start and people maintaining weight loss after a full course are the usual candidates. It does not bypass medical criteria. Claims that microdosing treats inflammation or aging are not backed by strong evidence.

    Low-Dose Schedule in Salisbury

    There is no single standard schedule. Providers often start below the usual first dose, such as a fraction of the 0.25 mg semaglutide or 2.5 mg tirzepatide starting dose, and increase in smaller steps than the label calls for. Most plans stay weekly. Any change should go through your provider first.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Early weeks vary a lot between people. At low doses, one person may feel full sooner by week two while another feels no difference until the dose is nudged up. Nausea is usually milder. Weight change tends to be slower and smaller than with full-dose treatment.

    Side Effects in Salisbury

    Lower doses reduce side effects but do not erase them. Nausea, constipation, diarrhea, reflux and reduced appetite are still the most common, especially in the days after any dose increase, even a small one. Serious risks still apply. Pancreatitis, gallbladder problems and the thyroid C-cell tumor warning from rodent studies apply at any dose.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening is the same as for full doses. The review covers BMI, weight history, diagnoses, medications and earlier GLP-1 use, with questions about the dose you reached and what made you stop. MEN 2 is a hard stop. Past pancreatitis or an eating disorder needs a longer conversation.

    Microdosing Compared With Standard GLP-1 Treatment in Salisbury

    Standard dosing has the trial data. Wegovy and Zepbound at full doses were tested in large clinical trials that showed average weight loss of about 15 to 20 percent, while microdosing has not been studied at that scale. Expect smaller average results. Maintenance is a common use case.
