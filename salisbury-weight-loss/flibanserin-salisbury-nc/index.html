    How Flibanserin Treatment Works for Women in Salisbury, NC

    How Flibanserin Works in Salisbury

    Flibanserin acts on serotonin receptors in the brain, not on blood flow and not on hormones. By turning down some serotonin activity, it is thought to give dopamine and norepinephrine, the chemicals linked with interest and motivation, more room to work. It is not a hormone. It is also nothing like Viagra, which changes blood flow and does little for desire itself. Effects build slowly over weeks of nightly use.

    Who Flibanserin Is Meant For

    The FDA approval covers premenopausal women with acquired, generalized HSDD. That means interest was fine before, it has faded across the board, and the change is causing you distress. Not every dip qualifies. If the main cause is an antidepressant, a thyroid problem or conflict at home, flibanserin is not the first answer. Postmenopausal women are not part of the labeled population.

    Dosing and the Nightly Schedule in Salisbury

    The dose is one 100 mg tablet, once a day, at bedtime. Always at bedtime. Dizziness and sleepiness peak in the hours right after a dose, so taking it before sleep means most of that happens while you are already asleep. This is a daily medication, not something taken before sex. If you forget a night, skip it and take the next dose the following bedtime.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly about side effects, usually mild sleepiness or dizziness that eases. That part usually passes. By weeks four to six, women who respond often notice they think about sex more and mind the low desire less. In the clinical trials, the average gain over placebo was modest, about one additional satisfying sexual event a month, along with less distress. Your provider checks in near week eight, and if nothing meaningful has changed, flibanserin is stopped.

    Side Effects of Flibanserin in Salisbury

    The most common side effects are dizziness, sleepiness, nausea and fatigue. Dry mouth and trouble sleeping show up too. The serious risk is a sharp drop in blood pressure that can cause fainting, and it is more likely after alcohol or with drugs that raise flibanserin levels. Plan on no driving for six hours after the tablet, which bedtime dosing mostly takes care of. Message your provider the same day if you get lightheaded or unusually drowsy.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with your full medication list. The big exclusion is any moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitor, a group that covers certain antifungals, antibiotics, HIV drugs and grapefruit juice in quantity. Women with any liver impairment cannot take it. Expect questions about past fainting, blood pressure, depression, pregnancy plans and weekly drinks.

    Flibanserin Compared With PT-141 and Other Options in Salisbury

    Flibanserin and bremelanotide, sold as Vyleesi and often called PT-141, are the two FDA-approved medicines for HSDD before menopause. One is a pill every night and the other is a shot only when you want it. Women who drink regularly sometimes lean toward PT-141 because the alcohol rules are simpler. Oxytocin therapy is compounded and used off-label, and it is not FDA-approved for desire. Your provider explains which fits.
