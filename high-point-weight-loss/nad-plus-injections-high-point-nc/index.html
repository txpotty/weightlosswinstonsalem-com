    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in High Point, NC

    How NAD+ Works in High Point

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. In every cell it carries electrons through the reactions that turn food into ATP, and it is consumed by sirtuins and PARPs, enzymes tied to DNA repair and stress responses. Older tissue tends to have less. A subcutaneous injection places NAD+ under the skin, where it is absorbed into the bloodstream without passing through the digestive tract, though how much reaches each tissue is still being studied.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Typical patients want steadier energy, clearer thinking or better recovery. Some add NAD+ during GLP-1 weight loss to support how they feel while eating less. It is not a weight loss medication and does not treat any specific disease, and people who are pregnant, breastfeeding, have active cancer or significant liver or kidney disease are generally not candidates.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in High Point

    Small shots, slow push. A common start is roughly 50 milligrams under the skin a few times weekly, with increases every week or two depending on how you feel. Use the belly or thigh with a fine needle. A slow injection makes a real difference.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. Many people feel warmth, flushing, mild nausea or a tight feeling right after injecting, and it usually fades within minutes. Over weeks two to four, some patients report steadier energy or clearer thinking, while others notice little change. Doses change based on your notes.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in High Point

    Flushing leads the list. Injection site redness, stinging and soreness, along with nausea, lightheadedness and headache, are also common. Chest tightness, muscle cramps or a racing heart can happen if the dose is too high or pushed too fast, and these usually improve with a lower dose or slower injection. Hives or trouble breathing mean call 911.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history and your supplement list. Expect questions on cancer, pregnancy, organ health, heart rhythm and whether you use NMN, NR or high-dose niacin. Some cases get a metabolic panel. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in High Point

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. An NAD+ drip delivers a larger amount in one sitting but can take two to four hours, and many people feel more nausea and cramping during the infusion. NAD+ nasal spray avoids needles at a smaller dose. NMN and NR capsules work differently, as building blocks.
